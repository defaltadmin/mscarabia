What are cookies?

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, the types of cookies we use, and how to manage your cookie settings.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when a website loads in your browser. They help ensure the website functions properly, enhance security, provide a better user experience, and analyze performance.

How do we use cookies?

Our website uses both first-party and third-party cookies. First-party cookies are necessary for the website to function and do not collect personally identifiable data. Third-party cookies help us understand website performance, track interactions, and deliver relevant content.

Manage cookie preferences

When you first visit our website, a consent banner allows you to accept or decline analytics cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site.

Additionally, you can control cookies through your browser settings. Below are links to manage cookies in major browsers:

  • Chrome: support.google.com
  • Safari: support.apple.com
  • Firefox: support.mozilla.org
  • Edge: support.microsoft.com
